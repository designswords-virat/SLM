Milestones That
Shaped Us

1954

Foundation Laid in Dust

Late Shri Sohan Lal Mathur laid the foundation of SLM, beginning with Rajasthan's earliest bitumen roads — the Mehla-Jobner and Tonk-Malpura stretches. First stones. First trust.

Mehla-Jobner · Tonk-Malpura
1960

Beyond Roads, Into Factories

Industrial entry via Rohtas Industries and Kamani Engineering Limited — SLM's first major pivot from public infrastructure into manufacturing-sector construction.

Rohtas Industries · Kamani Engineering
1970

Building for the Public

Started with government infrastructure: the Rajasthan Police Academy and the Open-Air Theatre at Ramniwas Bagh, among the earliest landmark civic assignments.

Rajasthan Police Academy · Ramniwas Bagh
1980

Hindustan Zinc & Birla Cement

Public works with Hindustan Zinc and Birla Cement — corporate offices, staff quarters, hospitals and the Birla Cement Airstrip at Chittorgarh.

Hindustan Zinc · Birla Corporation
1990

The Vanyavilas Benchmark

Partnering with leading architects, delivering landmark projects including Oberoi Vanyavilas — India's ultra-luxury wildlife resort benchmark in Ranthambore.

Oberoi Vanyavilas · Ranthambore
2000

Hotels & High-Rises

Hospitality boom — Gold Palace, Hotel Paradise (Ramada) — alongside multi-storey premium residential launches like Akshat Nilay in Civil Lines, Jaipur.

Gold Palace · Akshat Nilay
2010

Integrated Developments

Township-scale delivery — Akshat Meadows — paired with five-star resorts like The Westin Pushkar and Stardom, plus SEZ-grade industrial facilities at Sitapura.

Akshat Meadows · Westin Pushkar
2020

India's Largest Assignments

India's largest auto foundries, educational institutes and spiritual complexes — including the 17-storey Gupt Vrindavan Dham cultural complex at Jagatpura.

Gupt Vrindavan Dham · Jagatpura
